# Security policy

This policy covers every repository in the `dinetap-global` organisation and the Dinetap products built from them: the Dinetap app, Dinetap Hub, the admin web and `dinetap.com`.

## Report a vulnerability

Email **security@dinetap.com** with:

- what you found and where (a URL, an app version, or a file and line);
- the steps to reproduce it;
- what an attacker could do with it.

Please don't open a public issue or pull request for a vulnerability, and don't share it with others until we have fixed it.

## What to expect

- We confirm we received your report within 3 working days.
- We tell you whether we accept it, and how severe we judge it, within 10 working days.
- We fix accepted reports by severity: critical within 7 days, high within 30, medium within 90.
- We tell you when it is fixed, and credit you if you want to be named.

## Rules for testing

- Test only against accounts and data you own.
- Don't read, change or delete other people's data; stop and report as soon as you reach any.
- Don't run denial-of-service, spam or social-engineering tests, and don't test our vendors' systems (for example Stripe, Airwallex, Cloudflare).

We won't take legal action against research done in good faith under these rules.

## Personal data

Questions about personal data, or requests to see or delete your data, go to our Data Protection Officer at **dpo@dinetap.com**.
